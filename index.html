HOW TO PLAY
×
🎲 1. ROLL THE DICE Click the Roll Dice button to roll a die. Each roll adds to your current score.
⚠️ 2. WATCH OUT FOR 1 If you roll a 1, you lose all the points accumulated during your current turn, and the turn switches to the other player.
📥 3. HOLD YOUR POINTS Click Hold to add your current score to your total score. Your turn then passes to the other player.
🏆 4. WIN THE GAME The first player to reach 50 points wins the game!
🔄 NEW GAME Want a fresh start? Click New Game to reset the scores and begin again.
