# Pig Game 🎲

A simple two-player dice game I built using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

## What's the game about?

Pig is a two-player game where you take turns rolling a dice and trying to get to 50 points first.

The idea is simple: keep rolling to increase your score, but don't get too greedy because some rolls can cost you your points.

## How to Play

- Players take turns rolling the dice.
- Each roll adds to your current score.
- You can click the hold button to save your current score.
- Holding your score passes the turn to the other player.
- First player to reach 50 wins.

### The bad rolls

There are two rolls you don't want to get:

- **1** – You lose all the points accumulated in that round.
- **3** – Same thing, your current round score goes back to zero and you lose your turn.

So yeah, know when to stop rolling.
